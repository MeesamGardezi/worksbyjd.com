Why WORKS by JD

The Design-Build difference

Most contractors separate design and construction. We don't. Our integrated approach means faster timelines, lower costs, and zero finger-pointing between vendors.

01
True Design-Build

One team owns the entire project — concept to completion. No miscommunication between your designer and contractor. No surprises on bid day. Just a seamless process with a single accountable partner.

02
7-Year Workmanship Warranty

We stand behind every nail, tile, and finish we install — for 7 full years. That's 3–5× longer than industry standard, and it's not just a promise: it's written into every contract we sign.

03
Private Client Portal

Track your project in real time — photos, schedule updates, payment milestones, and direct messaging with your team. No more wondering what's happening at your home. Full transparency, every day.

04
NARI Certified Professionals

Jesse DeBenedictis holds four NARI certifications: CRPM, CR, CKBR, and CLC — among the industry's most rigorous credentials. Your home is in the hands of someone who has earned the right to touch it.

05
Zero Renovation Stress

We handle permits, schedules, subcontractors, and material procurement. Your job is to make decisions and trust the process. Our job is to make those decisions easy — and your home extraordinary.

06
North Shore's Trusted Team

Since 2001, we've built deep roots across Beverly, Gloucester, Rockport, Manchester-by-the-Sea, Hamilton, Essex, Salem, and the entire Cape Ann region. Our reputation is our biggest asset — and we protect it on every project.
