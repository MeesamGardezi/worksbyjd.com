Our Company

We've been building North Shore homes since 2001.

WORKS by JD was founded in Beverly, Massachusetts by Jesse DeBenedictis with a straightforward mission: deliver the quality of a custom home builder to homeowners doing large-scale renovation work. No subcontracting everything out. No disappearing project managers. One integrated team that owns the design and the build.

Over 24 years, we've completed hundreds of projects across the North Shore — from Beverly and Manchester-by-the-Sea to Gloucester, Rockport, Ipswich, and beyond. We've earned four NARI certifications, our Massachusetts CS and HIC licenses, and a reputation that lets most of our work come from referrals.

We're not the largest remodeler in the region. We're the most thorough. Every project gets Jesse's personal attention — the standards haven't changed since day one.
