Why WORKS by JD

What it's like to work here.

The Craft Comes First

We do a small number of projects per year and do them very well. We don't rush. We don't cut corners. The work we put into homes in Beverly, Rockport, and Gloucester lasts decades — and we're proud of it.

Real Accountability

Everyone at WORKS owns their lane. Your work gets seen, your judgment gets respected, and your growth is real. There's no "just follow orders" culture here — we want people who think and care.

Competitive Pay

We pay well because we hire the best. All positions are full-time, W-2, with competitive salary or hourly rates — not 1099 gig work. Year-round employment is our standard.

Growth & Credentials

We support NARI certifications and continuing education expenses for team members who pursue credentials. Jesse is NARI-certified himself and strongly believes in formal industry credentialing.

Great Clients

Our clients are engaged, appreciative, and invest seriously in their homes. You'll work on projects you're proud to show people — kitchens, bathrooms, and renovations that get photographed and recognized.

The North Shore

We work in some of the most beautiful towns in New England — Rockport, Manchester-by-the-Sea, Gloucester, Essex, Beverly. If you love this coastline, you'll love showing up for work.

How to Apply

A simple, respectful process.

01

Send a Message

Use the contact form and tell us the position you're interested in, a bit about yourself, and why WORKS by JD. No resume required to start — just a thoughtful note.

02

Phone Screen

We'll schedule a 20-minute phone call to understand your background, current situation, and work style. We're not using a checklist — we're having a real conversation.

03

Site Visit or Portfolio Review

Field candidates join us on a job site. Designers and PMs do a deeper conversation and portfolio or project review. You'll see how we work, who we are, and whether it fits.

04

Offer

If we're a match, we move fast — a written offer within a week of the site visit. We don't waste good people's time.
