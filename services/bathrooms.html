Luxury Bathroom Design-Build

Crafted for comfort. Built to impress.

A beautifully remodeled bathroom is one of the highest-ROI investments you can make in your home — and the one renovation clients tell us they use and appreciate every single day.

WORKS by JD specializes in luxury master bathroom remodels that combine spa-quality materials with thoughtful design. We've completed dozens of bathrooms across Beverly, Gloucester, Manchester-by-the-Sea, and the greater North Shore — each one uniquely tailored to the homeowner's style and lifestyle.

Our CKBR-certified team handles every aspect from layout redesign and tile selection through plumbing rough-in, heating systems, and final trim installation.
