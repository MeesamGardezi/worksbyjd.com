Deck Systems

Built for the coast. Built to last.

Coastal New England demands more from a deck than most climates. Salt air, freeze-thaw cycles, and high UV exposure will destroy a mediocre deck within a decade. We engineer every deck with proper footings, corrosion-resistant hardware, and materials proven to perform in this climate.

Our preferred composite materials (TimberTech, Trex, Fiberon) carry their own 25–30 year warranties on fading and staining. Combined with our 7-Year Workmanship Warranty, your investment is thoroughly protected.

Typical Range
$15K–$80K+
What We Build

From simple platforms to full deck systems.

🪵
Composite Decking

TimberTech, Trex, and Fiberon systems in premium wood-tone colors. Low maintenance, 25-year fade warranty, designed for coastal climates.

🌳
Hardwood & Ipe

Brazilian Ipe and cedar hardwood decks for clients who want authentic wood grain and a rich natural aesthetic that ages beautifully.

⚙️
Railings & Balusters

Cable rail systems, powder-coated aluminum, aluminum pickets, and glass panel options — code-compliant, beautiful, and durable.

🏛️
Pergolas & Overhead

Free-standing and attached pergola systems in cedar, Ipe, or aluminum, with optional motorized louvered roofs for all-weather coverage.

🔦
Integrated Lighting

In-deck LED riser lights, post cap lights, and string light systems wired to a smart controller — beautiful from sunset to midnight.

🔩
Structural Engineering

Engineered footings, ledger bolting to code, corrosion-resistant stainless and hot-dipped galvanized hardware throughout — no shortcuts.
