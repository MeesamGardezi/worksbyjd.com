Outdoor Living Spaces

Your backyard,
reinvented.

The North Shore has some of the most beautiful natural settings in New England — and outdoor living spaces that match that character can transform your home and lifestyle. Whether you're envisioning a multi-level deck, a fully-equipped outdoor kitchen, or a cozy fire pit gathering space, we design and build it all under one roof.

Every outdoor project is engineered for the New England climate — proper footings, moisture-resistant materials, and systems designed to perform through four seasons of coastal weather.

$30K–$200K+
What We Build

Outdoor living, completely covered.

🌿
Custom Decks & Patios

Multi-level composite and hardwood decks, bluestone and EP Henry paver patios, and mixed-material platforms designed for your lot and lifestyle.

🍖
Outdoor Kitchens & Bars

Built-in grills, pizza ovens, refrigerators, sinks, and bar areas with granite counters and weatherproof cabinetry — the full outdoor culinary experience.

🔥
Fire Features

Custom stone fire pits, gas fire tables, and outdoor fireplaces with seating walls — designed for year-round entertaining and warmth.

🏛️
Pergolas & Shade Structures

Cedar, Ipe, and powder-coated aluminum pergola systems with louvered roof options, ceiling fans, and string light integration.

💡
Lighting & Audio

Low-voltage landscape lighting, in-deck LED strips, outdoor speakers, and smart control systems that turn on at sunset automatically.

🌱
Landscape Integration

Retaining walls, planter boxes, privacy screens, and drainage solutions that make the hardscape feel connected to the landscape.
