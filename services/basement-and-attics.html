Basement & Attic Finishing

More space. No addition required.

Finishing a basement or attic is often the highest-ROI investment a homeowner can make — transforming unusable storage into functional, beautiful living space at a fraction of the cost of a full addition. The key is doing it right: proper vapor barriers, egress windows, insulation systems, electrical, mechanical, and a design that feels as intentional as the rest of your home.

We handle every element of the project under one roof — from structural assessment and moisture mitigation through framing, insulation, mechanical rough-in, finish work, and final inspection.

Technical Excellence

The unsexy work that makes all the difference.

  • ✓
    Moisture assessment first — we evaluate and address any basement moisture issues before framing begins, preventing future mold problems
  • ✓
    Egress windows — properly sized and installed to code for all habitable basement spaces; critical for safety and resale
  • ✓
    Spray foam & rigid insulation — proper thermal and vapor control systems rather than just batting in stud bays
  • ✓
    Full mechanical coordination — HVAC extension, plumbing rough-in, and electrical all coordinated through our trades team
  • ✓
    7-Year Workmanship Warranty — every element of the finish work covered unconditionally
