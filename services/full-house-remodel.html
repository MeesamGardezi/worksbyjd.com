Whole-Home Transformation

One team. One vision. No handoffs.

A full-house remodel is the most complex project a homeowner can undertake — and it demands a contractor with the experience, the systems, and the team depth to manage every discipline in concert. With WORKS by JD, you have one point of contact from design through delivery, and one team accountable for the entire outcome.

Whether you're gut-renovating a historic colonial in Rockport, reworking the floor plan of a Beverly cape, or doing a complete top-to-bottom transformation before move-in, our Design-Build process is built for exactly this kind of project.
