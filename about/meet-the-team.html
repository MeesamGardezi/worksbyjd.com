Meet the Team

The people who build your home right.

Designers, project managers, craftspeople, and craftswomen — every one of them chosen for the same reason: they meet the WORKS standard. No exceptions.

Leadership

The people who oversee every project.

JD
Jesse DeBenedictis
Founder & Principal
CRPM · CR · CKBR · CLC

Jesse founded WORKS by JD in 2001 after a decade in residential construction. He holds four NARI certifications and remains personally involved in every project from first consultation to final walkthrough. His personal phone number is in every client's contact file.

MR
Maria Reyes
Lead Designer
Interior Design · ASID Affiliate

Maria brings 15 years of residential interior design experience to every WORKS project. Her background in fine art and architecture inform a design aesthetic that is editorial, material-driven, and always grounded in how people actually live in their homes.

TH
Tom Hannigan
Senior Project Manager
CR · MA CS-88204

Tom has been managing WORKS projects for 12 years. A former lead carpenter himself, he understands field realities and manages schedules and subcontractors with a level of precision that consistently delivers projects on time and within budget.

EK
Elena Kowalski
Project Manager
PMP · NARI Member

Elena joined WORKS in 2018 and manages the client portal experience alongside project execution. Her background is in construction management, and she's known among clients for her communication style — proactive, clear, and always honest about where the project stands.

DM
Daniel Moretti
Lead Carpenter
CLC · 18 Years Field Experience

Daniel leads the finish carpentry and millwork team at WORKS. His custom cabinetry, built-in storage, and trim work represent the highest-quality output on the job site. When a project looks like a magazine, Daniel's hands made it happen.

AL
Angela Li
Design Coordinator
Interior Design · Tile & Stone Specialist

Angela manages material selection, tile and stone sourcing, fixture specifications, and finish coordination across all active projects. Her eye for material compatibility and detail is one of the key reasons WORKS projects have the cohesive look they do.

Our Craftspeople

The field team behind every project.

Behind every project is a team of skilled field craftspeople — tile setters, finish carpenters, painters, electricians, plumbers, and HVAC technicians — who meet the WORKS standard. Every trade partner is vetted personally by Jesse, carries full insurance, and has a track record of quality on WORKS job sites.

We don't use whoever's available. We use who's earned it.
