## Features

### Design & Build Process
- True Design-Build model — one team handles everything from concept to completion
- Client-driven process with input at every decision stage
- Private client portal for real-time project tracking

### Services Offered
- **Kitchen Remodeling** — Custom cabinetry, luxury finishes, full design-build
- **Bathroom Remodeling** — Luxury master baths, tile, custom vanities, radiant floor heating
- **Outdoor Living** — Custom decks/patios, outdoor kitchens & bars, fire pits, pergolas, integrated lighting & audio
- **Decking** — Composite and wood deck installation
- **Windows & Doors** — Exterior window and door installation/replacement
- **Basement & Attic Finishing**
- **Full House Remodels** — Complete whole-home renovations and floor plan reworks
- **Home Additions & Room Extensions**
- **Painting & Wallpapering** — Interior and exterior painting

### Trust & Credibility
- **7-Year Workmanship Warranty** (competitors typically offer 1–2 years)
- NARI Certified Professional — certifications: CRPM, CR, CKBR, CLC
- Licensed by Commonwealth of Massachusetts (CS #91245, HIC #178604)
- Fully insured with workers' compensation
