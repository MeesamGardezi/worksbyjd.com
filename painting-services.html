Painting Services

Prep is everything. We never skip it.

Most painting failures trace back to inadequate surface preparation — not the paint. Our crews sand, patch, prime, caulk, and mask before a single drop of finish coat goes on. On exterior work, we pressure wash, scrape, prime bare wood, and use elastomeric sealants to prevent future peeling.

We use premium paints exclusively — Benjamin Moore Aura, Sherwin-Williams Emerald, and exterior formulas engineered for New England conditions. The result is a finish that looks better and lasts longer than what most painting companies deliver.
