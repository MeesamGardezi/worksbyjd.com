Home Additions

The right addition changes everything.

Moving is expensive, stressful, and often unnecessary when a thoughtfully designed addition can give you exactly what you need — right in the home and neighborhood you already love. Our Design-Build process ensures the addition integrates with your existing structure architecturally, structurally, and aesthetically.

We handle everything: the design and engineering, zoning and variance navigation, permitting, structural work, mechanical coordination, and all interior and exterior finish work. One contract. One team. Zero handoffs.

Typical Range
$80K–$400K+
Types of Additions

Whatever space you need, we can build it.

🏠
Room Additions

Attached room additions that expand your living room, master suite, or family room — blending seamlessly with your existing roofline and architecture.

↔️
Bump-Outs

Smaller, cost-effective extensions (4–10 feet) that dramatically expand a kitchen, bathroom, or bedroom without the cost of a full addition.

👨‍👩‍👧
In-Law Suites

Fully self-contained in-law apartments and accessory dwelling units — private entrance, kitchenette, bedroom, and bath, designed for independence and dignity.

⬆️
Second-Floor Additions

Complete second-floor additions over a first-floor footprint — bedrooms, baths, and living space added above without expanding your lot coverage.

🌞
Sunrooms & Four-Season Rooms

Glass-and-frame sunroom additions with radiant floor heating, Andersen windows, and insulated ceilings — usable year-round on the North Shore.

🚗
Garage Conversions

Converting attached garages into finished living space — offices, studios, gyms, or family rooms — with proper insulation and systems integration.

Our Process

How a WORKS addition gets built.

  • 1
    Feasibility & Zoning Review — we assess setbacks, lot coverage limits, and zoning rules before you commit to anything
  • 2
    Architectural Design — our in-house designers develop full plans that integrate with your existing home architecturally and structurally
  • 3
    Engineering & Permitting — structural engineering, permit applications, and coordination with the building department managed entirely by our team
  • 4
    Construction — foundation, framing, roofing, exterior cladding, all mechanicals, and complete interior finish work
  • 5
    Final Inspection & Delivery — we manage the certificate of occupancy process and final walkthrough — you don't lift a finger
