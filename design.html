07Design principles
01
Craft over flash
Every surface should feel hand-built, not mass-produced. Warmth from the Ember palette, weight from clay shadows. This is a premium craftsman brand — the UI must earn that trust.
02
Editorial type
Playfair Display for all display and headings — it carries authority and craftsmanship. Inter for UI and body copy. Italic Playfair for brand voice moments and pull quotes. Never use Inter for headlines.
03
Warm, not rustic
Ember orange is the hero — used for primary actions, accents, and brand moments. Stone neutrals provide warmth without competing. Forge charcoal anchors the dark surfaces. Never use cool greys.
04
Trust is visible
7-Year Warranty, NARI certs, MA license numbers — these are competitive advantages and must be prominent, not buried. Credibility components are first-class UI, not footnotes.
05
Photo-first
Portfolio photography is the product. The UI is the frame — never compete with project photos. Use dark overlays on hero images, minimal chrome in gallery views, and let the craftsmanship speak.
06
Conversion is the goal
Every page leads toward "Start a Project." CTAs are always Ember Primary. The free consultation offer is the hook — surface it everywhere. One clear action per screen beats three competing ones.
