Beverly Palmer — Master Bath

A complete master bathroom transformation in Beverly, MA — from dated tile to a spa-worthy retreat with radiant floors, soaking tub, and a custom multi-head steam shower.

Investment
$85K
Duration
10 weeks
Cert
CKBR
The Project

A personal spa. Built to last.

Sarah and Tom Palmer came to us with a master bathroom that hadn't been touched since the 1990s. The layout was inefficient, the tile was dated, and there was no tub despite the size of the room. They had a clear vision: they wanted a spa. We helped them define exactly what that meant — and then built it.

The floor plan was reconfigured to accommodate a freestanding 67" soaking tub positioned beneath the window, a new custom double vanity opposite, and a relocated shower expanded to 4×6 feet with a built-in bench and three body sprays. Radiant electric heating was installed under the full floor, topped with Calcutta Gold large-format porcelain.

The vanity was custom-built in-house: shaker doors in warm white, a double-tier white oak floating medicine cabinet with integrated lighting, and a leathered white quartz countertop. Plumbing fixtures throughout are Brizo Levoir in matte black.

The shower features a floor-to-ceiling Calcutta tile surround, a recessed niche lit from above, and frameless ⅜" glass with a custom European pull handle. The complete package is a master bath that exceeds what most dedicated spa facilities offer.

"I take my time getting ready every morning now because the bathroom is so beautiful. We spent a lot on this project but not a single day goes by where we don't appreciate it."

Sarah P.
