Tewksbury — Master Bathroom Remodel

A complete master bathroom gut renovation in Tewksbury — radiant floors, custom glass tile shower, a dual vanity with quartz counters, and a freestanding soaking tub.

The Project

Modern warmth. Lasting quality.

David and Michelle were clear about what they wanted: a master bath that felt warm, modern, and completely different from the 1980s tile-and-tub setup they'd been living with. They didn't want sterile minimalism — they wanted something that felt personal and inviting. We delivered exactly that.

The project began with a full gut — down to the studs and subfloor. We installed a Schluter waterproof system before any tile work, ensuring the shower and wet areas would remain moisture-free for decades. Radiant electric heat was installed under the full floor, topped with a large-format 24×24 warm taupe porcelain that flows seamlessly from the floor throughout the shower floor as well.

The shower is the design focal point — a custom glass tile accent wall in a linear mosaic, flanked by the same large-format porcelain on the other walls. A semi-frameless glass enclosure with matte black hardware completes the look. The rainfall showerhead is ceiling-mounted, centered above a built-in teak tray bench.

The dual vanity is a custom piece with shaker-style doors in a greige-toned finish, quartz countertops in Silestone Eternal Calacatta with subtle movement, and undermount rectangular sinks. Matte black faucets tie to the shower hardware. A large format flat-panel mirror spans the full vanity width with integrated lighting above.

"Working with WORKS was the easiest home project experience we've had. The Client Portal kept us in the loop without constant back-and-forth, and the finished bathroom is beyond what we imagined."

David & Michelle
Tewksbury, MA
