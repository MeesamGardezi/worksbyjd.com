Gloucester Cahill — Master Bath

A bold spa-inspired master bathroom in Gloucester featuring heated Calacatta marble, a custom floating double vanity, a wet room steam shower, and a freestanding sculptural tub.

Investment
$92K
Duration
12 weeks
Cert
CKBR
The Project

Coastal luxury. Uncompromised.

Michael and Rachel T. had renovated their Gloucester home room by room over several years. The master bathroom was the last major project, and they were clear: they wanted it to be the showpiece. They described it as a "five-star hotel bath that feels like ours." That's exactly what we built.

The floor plan was opened by removing the separate shower stall and creating a full wet room — shower and tub sharing a continuous tiled space. The floor is Calacatta marble mosaic with radiant heat below; the walls are 24×48 bookmatched slabs in honed Calacatta Borghini. The effect is seamless, sculptural, and warm.

The double vanity spans the full wall at 96 inches — a custom floating piece in matte charcoal lacquer with an integrated stone shelf and two undermount sinks. A back-lit mirror spans the full width above. Kohler Purist fixtures throughout in brushed nickel, with a wall-mount rain head and three body jets in the shower.

The freestanding tub is positioned as a centerpiece — a Kohler Abrazo in white matte with a free-standing tub filler in brushed nickel. It's flanked by the window looking out to the yard, with frosted lowers for privacy. The lighting above is architectural — indirect LED coves around the ceiling perimeter with warm-toned dimming.

"Our friends assumed we installed a whole new addition when they saw it. It's barely recognizable from what it was. WORKS by JD exceeded every expectation."

Michael & Rachel T.
Gloucester, MA
