The Project

A home reborn.

James and Karen L. purchased this 1920s colonial in Rockport knowing it would need a complete transformation. What they didn't expect was how thoroughly we could reimagine the space while preserving the character that made them fall in love with it.

The scope touched every room in the home. We opened the first floor, removed the wall between the kitchen and dining room, relocated a load-bearing wall with engineered steel, and created a flowing open plan that finally reflected how a family actually lives. Every system was replaced: 200-amp electrical, new gas line, new high-efficiency HVAC, full plumbing repiping, and new spray foam insulation throughout.

The kitchen was built around a 14-foot island with leathered quartzite, custom inset cabinetry in a warm sage, a 48" dual-fuel range, and a handmade ceramic tile backsplash. Three bathrooms followed: a master with heated Calacatta marble floors and a custom steam shower, a hall bath in crisp white subway with a hex floor, and a new first-floor powder room.

Outside, we replaced all windows and exterior doors, new cedar shingle siding, painted the trim Chantilly Lace, and built a three-level composite deck system off the rear — connected to the new sliding glass doors we installed in the dining room.

"We told Jesse we wanted a magazine home. He delivered something better — a home that actually works for how we live, and somehow also looks like a magazine."

James & Karen L.
Rockport, MA
